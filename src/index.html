02 / In the workshop

Equipment lending

Know who has what. Record checkouts and returns, then surface overlapping loans when devices exchange their histories.

Open equipment lending ↗
01. Load before you leave.Open both apps once with a connection. Records stay in this browser.
02. Work on each device.The desk keeps recording while disconnected. Separate browsers have separate records.
03. Carry the update.Show the QR or play its sequence while the other device scans. Review the handoff before importing.

Keep the evidence. Show the conflict.

Disconnected devices cannot know what the other has done. DeadAir retains both histories and highlights conflicting equipment loans after handoff. Operators decide which loan to return.

Working prototype · Camera scanning and multi-part QR are implemented; physical-device verification is pending. Clearing browser storage removes local records; export a backup first. Records are not sent to a server.
